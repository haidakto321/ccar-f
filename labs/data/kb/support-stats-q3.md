# Support stats, Q3 2026 (July - September)

Source: support analytics dashboard export. Date: 2026-10-05.

- Refund requests: 1,240 in total.
- 88% arrived within 30 days of delivery.
- 12% (149 requests) arrived between day 31 and day 45. All were refused or given store credit.
- Of those 149 customers, 41% did not order again in the next 60 days (all customers: 18%).
- Average refund value: $74. Refund fraud rate: 0.6% of refunds.
- Top reason for late requests: "gift, opened late" (52%).
