# Internal refund policy

Source: internal policy wiki, owner Customer Ops. Last updated: 2026-01-10.

- Customers can ask for a refund within **30 days of delivery**.
- Refunds above $500 need approval from a human supervisor.
- Damaged-in-shipping items are always refunded or replaced, return shipping is free.
- After 30 days we offer store credit only, at the supervisor's choice.
